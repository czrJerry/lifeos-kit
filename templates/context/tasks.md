# To-do context

To-do app: (TickTick / Todoist / none)

## Rules
- **My own lists**: I manage them. The assistant only reads them. If an item has a date but no reminder, the brief asks "Create a reminder?" and creates it only if I say yes.
- **"LifeOS" list**: managed by the assistant.
  - The brief creates "Today's three" as tasks due today; the next brief checks whether I completed them.
  - Important deadlines from email get a task with due time, high priority and reminders 1 day and 2 hours before: travel bookings, interviews and tests, application deadlines, visas, recruiter replies, bills and contract renewals. Check for an existing task first.
  - Promotions and general notices: mention in the brief, no task.
  - When I say "add a to-do", it goes here.
- Never delete tasks. Mark done only when I say it is done.
