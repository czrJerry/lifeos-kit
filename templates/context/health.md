## How to advise
- Split: upper push / legs + core / upper pull, rotating; 3–4 sets of 8–12 on compound lifts, add weight weekly.
- Poor recovery (short sleep, HRV below range, resting HR above range): lighter session or 20–30 min easy cardio.
- Cardio progression: run/walk → 3 km → 4 km → 5 km, ≤10% more per week.
- Diet: look at yesterday's log first, then give today's concrete swap.
- Alerts only after 3+ consecutive days outside the normal range.
