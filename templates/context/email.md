## Priority rules for the brief
1. Anything with a deadline: tests, interviews, travel changes, complaint time limits.
2. Job search: real human replies > test/interview invites > application confirmations > rejections (one line) > job alerts (only those matching `profile.md`).
3. Side income opportunities.
4. Ongoing disputes with a new reply.
5. Travel: daily from 3 days before departure; changes on the day go to the top.
6. Money: bills, rate or terms changes, unusual transactions (one line each). No marketing.
7. Finance/news: one line.
